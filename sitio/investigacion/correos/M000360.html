6 ways to capture notes
-----------------------

Evernote makes capturing ideas and organizing important content more intuitive than ever. Here are six ways you can save everything in Evernote:

✍️ Create and type a note
-----------------------------

Quickly jot down thoughts, use a template for a ready-made structure, or design your note's layout to your liking. Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

📸 Snap a photo
-----------------

Evernote can read information in your documents, handwritten notes, or whiteboard diagrams—simply take a photo and save it in Evernote, and easily find it later. Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

🎙️ Record and transcribe audio
-----------------------------------

Capture, save, and transcribe your voice memos and meeting recordings directly in Evernote. Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

✉️ Forward emails
---------------------

Send emails directly to Evernote to keep all your important information organized in one place. Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

🔗 Clip the web
------------------

Clip any content from the web, like an interesting article or your new favorite recipe, and add it to your Evernote account with one tap. Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

📱 Save via mobile extension
------------------------------

Save content from other apps on iOS or Android directly into Evernote using your device's native sharing menu. Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

Once your notes are captured, organize them in a way that works for you by tagging them and grouping them into notebooks and spaces. Get more tips here ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] ).
