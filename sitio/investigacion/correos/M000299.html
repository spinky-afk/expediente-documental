Work better, together
*********************

Collaborating has never been simpler. Evernote makes it easy to share content and work together in real time, whether it’s a business proposal, your family vacation plan, or exam revision.

🤝 Share in seconds
----------------------

Start collaborating in real time on any note, notebook, or space with just a few clicks: send an email invitation or share a direct link, and get started straight away.

🔒 Set the right permissions
------------------------------

Decide who can view, edit, and invite others to your note. If things get messy, you can always change or revoke access.

🔑 Open collaboration
-----------------------

Want to share your content with the world? With the Lite Editor, anyone can view or edit your work in their browser, no Evernote account required. Perfect for publishing and sharing content online quickly—just set your preferences, share a link, and you're live!
