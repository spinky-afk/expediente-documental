Make Evernote yours
*******************

Whether you’re managing a work project, coordinating a class assignment, or jotting down personal notes, you can customize Evernote to match your workflow.

Here’s how to personalize your Evernote:

------------------------------
Notebooks, Stacks, and Spaces.
------------------------------

Leverage Evernote's simple organization system to stay organized. Add related notes to a notebook, stack notebooks together, and create spaces to group related content.

Learn more ( [URL histórica en evernote.com; consultar original] )

----
Tags
----

Label your notes with tags, so you can flexibly categorize, filter, and find content using keywords that make sense to you.

Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

----------
Navigation
----------

Show, hide, and reorder the sections in your menus to suit how you get around Evernote, on desktop and mobile.

Choose what works best for you! Whether you use popular methods like PARA or GTD ( [URL histórica en evernote.com; consultar original] ), or prefer to go with your own flow, Evernote helps you set things up your way.

To learn more personalization tips from Evernote’s Product Lead Federico Simionato, watch this video ( [URL histórica en youtu.be; consultar original] ).
