AI-powered productivity is here
*******************************

Evernote is smarter than ever! Built-in AI tools help you capture, understand, and act on your ideas faster, so you spend less time managing them and more time putting them into action.

Here’s how you can take advantage of the new AI features in Evernote—just make sure you’re using Evernote V11! If not, update the app first to access them.

Explore Evernote’s AI features ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

------------
AI Assistant
------------

Your personal productivity partner. Ask AI Assistant questions or make requests, and get personalized replies with helpful information from the web. You can even ask it to summarize content, organize notes, or improve your writing.

Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

---------------
Semantic Search
---------------

Go beyond what you type in your search bar—Semantic Search shows you all the notes and content that could be related, and highlights what’s most relevant.

Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

----------------
AI Meeting Notes
----------------

Record an in-person, online, or hybrid meeting and automatically generate a transcript with speaker recognition. Then, add a quick summary with action points in one click.

Learn more ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )

--------------------
AI Transcribe & Edit
--------------------

Spend less time typing by turning your handwritten notes and voice memos into text automatically.

Instantly polish, shorten, rephrase, or adjust the tone of your writing.

Learn more of AI Transcribe ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] ) and AI Edit ( [URL histórica en help.evernote.com; consultar original] )
